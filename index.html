      📘 इस्तेमाल कैसे करें (Hindi Usage Guide)

      1. Step 1: "New Analysis" tab mein apni basic brand details bharein — naam, niche, target audience aur tagline.
      2. Step 2: "+ Platform Add Karein" par click karke apne saare active platforms (Instagram, LinkedIn, YouTube, Fiverr, Upwork, etc.) unke followers aur posting frequency ke saath add karein.
      3. Step 3: Content pillars, visual consistency checklist, engagement details aur monetization streams bharein — jitna sach-sach bharenge, result utna accurate hoga.
      4. Step 4: "ANALYZE MY BRAND" button dabayein — turant aapko 0-100 ka Brand Score, category-wise radar chart, strengths/weaknesses aur personalized action plan mil jayega.
      5. Step 5: "Save Analysis" se result save karein — History tab mein aap apni progress time ke saath track kar sakte hain.
      6. Tip: Har mahine ek baar analysis repeat karein aur dekhen ki score improve ho raha hai ya nahi. Weak areas par diye gaye action items follow karein.
